# Examiner Summary: Proposed Breast-Cancer/FedProx Thesis

**Assessment posture:** This one-page summary is a claim-control aid for the source-only thesis package. It identifies what the record supports, what it does not support, and why the proposed study remains closed. It is not an empirical abstract, protocol approval, data-use authorization, clinical assessment, privacy assessment, or deployment report.

> **Core conclusion for review:** The thesis contributes a documentation-first boundary that distinguishes a proposed FedProx study from an executed empirical result. It does **not** report dataset use, model training, performance, clinical relevance, privacy outcome, hospital integration, deployment, or runtime behavior.

## Research question and bounded contribution

The thesis asks whether a synthetic, descriptor-only FedProx study-intent boundary can make the distinction between a proposed breast-cancer/FedProx study and an executed result explicit before any data, model, training, evaluation, update, submission, or aggregation action begins. The contribution is therefore one of **traceability, default closure, and claim discipline**. It preserves a chronological record that makes the absence of empirical evidence visible rather than filling the gap with assumed results.

## Evidence at a glance

| Area | What the record supports | What the record does not support |
|---|---|---|
| Thesis framing | A source-only research question, methodology, limitation structure, and claim-safe manuscript package. | An executed experiment or scientific performance result. |

| Dataset posture | Every supplied candidate is recorded as `ineligible` pending independent evidence. | Dataset acquisition, opening, copying, inspection, processing, storage, transfer, or use. |
| FedProx context | Literature-positioned rationale for a future proposed protocol. | A FedProx implementation result, comparison, metric, convergence claim, or clinical conclusion. |

## Data-governance stop condition

No empirical step is permitted by the current record. Before a future data-use decision can be considered, an accountable independent authority must establish the exact study purpose, primary-source provenance and rights chain, license conditions, relevant ethics and data-protection requirements, data-management and isolation design, and a prespecified study/evaluation/reporting protocol. Until that happens, each supplied candidate remains **ineligible**, and the proposed study remains **unexecuted**.

No dataset asset has been downloaded, opened, copied, processed, stored, transferred, trained on, inferred from, or evaluated. The documentation site and source-only Hospital Node Agent cannot be treated as a data intake or study-execution route.

## How the thesis should be read

The central value of the work is methodological restraint. The thesis makes its evidence state reviewable across Chapters 1–6: it gives context for FedProx, describes source-only controls, reports bounded local findings, states explicit non-results, and routes future work through independent governance. External literature informs context only; it does not transfer external results to this project.

The thesis must retain statements equivalent to the following:

> “The study is proposed and has not accessed a dataset, model, or empirical evaluation path.”

> “The documented local controls are not evidence of patient-data detection, privacy, clinical benefit, hospital integration, deployment, or runtime behavior.”

> “Future empirical work is conditional on independently evidenced governance, protocol, and technical decisions.”

| Does the manuscript avoid unsupported clinical, privacy, performance, or deployment language? | Evidence matrix, consistency audit, citation-and-claim-boundary audit, and editorial handoff. |
| Is the future empirical path framed as conditional and independently governed? | Readiness protocol, governance dossier, decision worksheet, supervisor handoff, and blank protocol checklist. |

## Final assessment boundary

The record supports assessment of the thesis as a **source-only governance, traceability, and claim-discipline contribution**. It does not support assessment of a breast-cancer model’s performance or clinical value because no model or dataset study has been performed. Any later empirical contribution would require a new independently governed protocol and a separate evidence record; it cannot be inferred from the present package.
