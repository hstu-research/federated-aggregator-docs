# Final Thesis Consistency Checklist: Source-Only Breast-Cancer/FedProx Package

**Review scope:** This documentation-only checklist reviews claim consistency across the integrated manuscript and the linked governance records named below. It is not an independent academic assessment, authorization, rights analysis, ethics review, data-protection decision, dataset inspection, model review, clinical assessment, privacy assessment, deployment review, or runtime proof.

> **Retained position:** The study is proposed and unexecuted. Every supplied dataset candidate remains `ineligible` for data use. No dataset asset, model, training run, inference output, metric, update, submission, aggregation, deployment, runtime artifact, clinical activity, or privacy outcome exists in this branch.

## 1. Documents within this consistency review

| Record group | Documents covered | Intended consistency function |
|---|---|---|
| Core thesis framing | Integrated six-chapter manuscript and examiner summary. | Preserve source-only contribution, explicit non-results, and conditional future work. |
| Evidence boundaries | Evidence matrix, marker-rejection completion audit, and chronological dossier. | Keep deterministic local controls distinct from data, privacy, clinical, operational, or runtime evidence. |
| Data-governance record | Public metadata review, governance dossier, blank decision worksheet, and supervisor handoff. | Preserve every candidate’s `ineligible` status and separate preparation from authority. |
| Study-preparation record | Blank protocol-registration checklist, amendment log, and thesis governance appendix crosswalk. | Keep study design, approval, amendment, and execution unassigned and non-executing. |

## 2. Completed documentation consistency checks

The entries below are editorial findings for the named documentation set. They record whether the published record uses aligned boundary language; they do not certify truth beyond the listed documents or substitute for independent review.

| Check | Expected condition | Editorial finding | Boundary retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| Proposal versus empirical result | The thesis must state that the study is proposed and not report a completed empirical study. | **Consistent in reviewed record.** The manuscript, examiner summary, crosswalk, and protocol checklist state a proposed, unexecuted posture. | No performance, comparison, metric, or statistical result is claimed. |
| Dataset status | The public-page review cannot become asset access or data-use permission. | **Consistent in reviewed record.** The review, governance dossier, worksheet, and handoff retain all candidates as `ineligible`. | No download, opening, copying, processing, storage, transfer, training, inference, or evaluation is claimed. |
| Authority and governance | Blank preparation records cannot be described as approval. | **Consistent in reviewed record.** The worksheet, supervisor handoff, protocol checklist, and amendment log are explicitly non-authoritative. | No rights, license, consent, ethics, data-protection, or institutional approval is claimed. |
| Local-control meaning | Deterministic generated-marker controls must not be described as patient-data or operational controls. | **Consistent in reviewed record.** The completion audit, thesis materials, and dossier limit the controls to source-only deterministic evidence. | No patient-data detection, data-loss prevention, privacy proof, hospital integration, or runtime capability is claimed. |
| FedProx interpretation | External FedProx knowledge may frame a future study but not supply project results. | **Consistent in reviewed record.** FedProx is described as proposed protocol context only. | No implementation, convergence, robustness, utility, clinical, or comparative-performance claim is made for this project. |
| Results and limitations | Non-results must remain visible, not implied away by future-work language. | **Consistent in reviewed record.** The manuscript structure, examiner summary, and crosswalk retain explicit non-results and conditional sequencing. | Future empirical work is neither approved nor started. |

| Chronology | Later records must not rewrite earlier evidence as stronger. | **Consistent in reviewed record.** Dossier entries distinguish design, documentation, local-control, and absent empirical/operational evidence. | The chronological record remains the decision authority for this branch. |

## 3. Required wording checks before thesis submission

| Topic | Required wording pattern | Disallowed implication |
|---|---|---|
| Dataset candidates | “Public page metadata reviewed only”; “ineligible pending independent evidence.” | That a dataset was used, licensed for the study, consented, de-identified, safe, or clinically suitable. |
| Governance documents | “Blank,” “preparatory,” “non-authoritative,” or “pending independent review.” | That a form, worksheet, handoff, or checklist constitutes approval. |
| Source-only controls | “Deterministic generated-marker control” and “bounded local evidence.” | That a control handled patient data, stopped a real upload, delivered privacy, or integrated with a hospital system. |
| FedProx | “Proposed protocol context” or “future conditional study.” | That FedProx was trained, compared, evaluated, or shown effective in this project. |
| Future work | “Conditional on independently evidenced authority, rights, governance, protocol, and technical decisions.” | That future work is guaranteed, funded, authorized, or underway. |

## 4. Open conditions that must not be closed by editorial review

Editorial consistency does not resolve any external condition. The following remain unassigned or unresolved and therefore retain the closed data-use and non-execution posture: responsible authority, primary-source provenance and rights, license sufficiency, consent/ethics/data-protection conditions, approved isolation and data-management environment, candidate-specific data-use eligibility, prespecified executable study protocol, model specification, evaluation plan, and independent sign-off.

## 5. Final editorial sign-off prompt

Before a thesis copy is presented, the writer and reviewer should confirm that every assertion is within its named evidence category and that no table, figure, title, abstract, conclusion, slide, or oral statement expands the boundary. A discrepancy must be recorded as a new documentation item; it must not be repaired by retrospective wording, undeclared scope change, or an unsupported result claim.

This checklist records an aligned documentation package only. It does not change the study status, candidate eligibility, or scope of evidence.
