# Synthetic FedProx Thesis Defense Narrative and Anticipated-Question Brief

**Status:** Thesis-defense preparation artifact. This brief helps present the documented source-only contribution accurately. It is not a presentation of empirical, clinical, privacy, performance, operational, deployment, or runtime results.

## Core defense narrative

### Opening

This thesis addresses a specific research-governance problem: a proposed synthetic FedProx protocol can be mistaken for an executed empirical result when the evidence boundary is not explicit. The work therefore focuses on distinguishing **study intent** from **study execution** before any trainer, dataset, model, metric, update, submission, or aggregation action is present.

### Research question

The question is whether a descriptor-only synthetic FedProx study-intent declaration can distinguish a proposed breast-cancer/FedProx protocol from an executed empirical result before any empirical action occurs.

### Method in one minute

The method is documentation-first and source-only. Exact frozen declarations represent non-executing study intent. Validators reject malformed, broadened, mutable, unknown, and replayed input. A one-use consumer, terminal in-memory closure, and disabled application fake preserve an all-gates-unassigned, ineligible, non-evaluating posture. The work is validated through deterministic fixtures, protected-import checks, and local source-quality gates.

### Result in one minute

The result is a bounded contract finding: the local controls distinguish proposed study intent from execution-shaped input and preserve terminal closure without creating an evaluation path. The branch also produces traceability, thesis evidence, governance-handoff, and manuscript-assembly artifacts that constrain how the work may be described.

### Limitation in one minute

No dataset, patient record, medical image, model artifact, training run, inference, metric calculation, update, aggregation event, remote workflow query, hospital system, clinical workflow, deployment, or runtime proof was included. Therefore, the thesis does not report FedProx performance, privacy in operation, clinical utility, hospital integration, or deployment readiness.

### Closing

The contribution is accountable distinction, not a performance claim. It creates a documented boundary that keeps a proposed federated-learning study from being presented as an empirical result and defines the independently governed decisions required before such a study could begin.

## Anticipated questions and grounded answers

| Likely question | Grounded answer | Do not say |
|---|---|---|
| Why use FedProx? | FedProx provides the proposed protocol context for studying how protocol intent is distinguished from execution. | That FedProx outperformed another method in this project. |
| Did you train a model? | No. The branch is source-only and has no trainer, dataset, model artifact, or empirical evaluation. | That a synthetic fixture is a trained model. |
| What are the results? | The results concern local contract behavior: exact non-executing intent, terminal closure, replay suppression, default-closed readiness, and disabled projection. | Accuracy, loss, F1, AUC, or convergence values. |
| Why is this relevant to breast-cancer research? | It provides a governance boundary for a proposed breast-cancer/FedProx research context, where overclaiming an unexecuted study would be inappropriate. | That it improves diagnosis or benefits patients. |
| Did you validate privacy? | No privacy outcome was empirically assessed. The work documents data and action prohibitions only. | That source-only controls prove privacy in operation. |
| Is this deployable? | No. No concrete adapter, connection, deployment, or runtime proof exists. | That local source quality is deployment certification. |
| What is next? | Independently governed authority, stewardship, protocol, evaluation, safety, reporting, and focused adapter-review decisions must precede any empirical work. | That a study is approved, scheduled, or ready to start. |
