## Generic-template boundaries

The template is intentionally professional and generic. It does not purport to be an approved template for a particular institution, and it does not invent a candidate identity, degree, department, institution, supervisor, examiner, acknowledgement, signature, date, or approval statement. Each placeholder must be completed only with verified information and with the official wording required by the relevant institution.

| Template area | Included treatment | Required user action |
|---|---|---|
| Title page | Formal thesis title, research subtitle, degree field, and identity/institution placeholders. | Replace placeholders with verified details. |
| Preliminary pages | Declaration, approval, and acknowledgements placeholders. | Use official institutional language or remove non-required sections. |
| Contents and abbreviations | Editable contents field and a short terminology list. | Update the contents field after heading edits and apply local style rules. |
| Main manuscript | Integrated source-only thesis with chapter hierarchy, tables, and references. | Retain the existing claim scope during any formatting or copy-editing work. |
